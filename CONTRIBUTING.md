# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list accurate. The aim is a practical, source-checked list of directories where an AI tool, SaaS product or startup can actually be listed — not a traffic ranking, and not a disguised advert.

This repository is connected to [GearPicked](https://gearpicked.com/submit/), which offers a paid manual-submission service to many of these directories. That does not change the bar for inclusion. Please say so in your pull request if you work for a directory, a submission service (including GearPicked), or a competing list.

## What to add or change

Useful pull requests:

- A dead or redirected URL, with the current destination.
- A cost, badge or eligibility change, with a link to the directory's own page and the date you checked it.
- A genuine new directory that meets the inclusion criteria below.
- A typo, unclear note, or a directory filed in the wrong category.

Please do not add:

- Domain Rating, traffic or "AI citation" figures unless you can point to an independent, dated source and are prepared for them to go stale quickly. This list currently omits DR for that reason.
- Directories whose only free route requires a mandatory badge or backlink on the listed site.
- Generic web directories, local citation sites, or "submit to 5,000 directories" packages.
- Coming-soon pages, parked domains, or a second URL for an operator already on the list.
- Paid promotional placements presented as editorial entries.

## Inclusion criteria

A directory belongs here if, on its own public site:

1. There is a current listing, launch or vendor-profile route (a form, documented email process, or equivalent).
2. The product types in scope are AI tools, SaaS / web apps, developer tools, or comparable software — not only local businesses or generic "website" listings.
3. The checked free route does not require a badge or backlink on your site. Paid-only directories may be included, with the cost labelled clearly.
4. The site is a going concern, not a stub, a temporary landing page, or a redirect already represented under another name.

Please check the page yourself. Do not copy cost or link-type claims from another round-up.

## How to submit a change

1. Open a pull request that edits `directories.csv` and the matching section of `README.md` together, so they do not drift.
2. In the PR body, include the directory's own source URL, what you changed, and the date and timezone of your check.
3. Keep notes short and factual. Do not promise traffic, approvals, dofollow links or ranking results.
4. If you are affiliated with the directory or with a submission service, say so in the PR.

New entries should include: name, homepage URL, official submit or pricing URL, category (`AI catalog`, `SaaS`, `startup launch board`, or `review site`), a suggested priority tier (1, 2 or 3 — this is a starting-order judgment, not a quality score), an access label (`Free`, `Free + paid options`, `Paid / fee applies`, or `Not stated`), the raw cost text from the source page, a short note, and the check date.

Tier guidance, to stay consistent with the existing list:

- **Tier 1** — broad-discovery starting points: well-known AI catalogs, launch workflows, alternative-finders or review marketplaces with a substantial public catalog and clear owner/launch guidance.
- **Tier 2** — a browsable catalog or launch feed plus a public plan, eligibility rule or review process. Sensible after the closest audience-fit destinations.
- **Tier 3** — narrower, smaller, or thinly documented routes. Read the unknowns first.

If you are unsure of the tier, propose one and say why; a maintainer can adjust it.

## Tone

Write like a careful researcher. "Not stated" is better than a guess. "Optional paid upgrade" is better than "cheap backlinks." This list should still be useful if GearPicked disappeared tomorrow.
